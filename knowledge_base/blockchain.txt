Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger technology that records transactions across multiple computers in a network. Information is organized into blocks, and each block is connected to the previous block through cryptographic references. This structure makes historical records difficult to modify without detection when the network is properly designed and maintained.

Blockchain networks can operate through different consensus mechanisms, which help participants agree on the validity and ordering of transactions. Proof of Work and Proof of Stake are two well-known consensus mechanisms. Smart contracts are programs stored and executed on compatible blockchain networks. They automatically perform predefined actions when specified conditions are satisfied. Blockchain technology is used in digital assets, supply chain tracking, decentralized applications, and certain digital identity systems. Its limitations include transaction costs, scalability challenges, regulatory considerations, and energy consumption in some network designs.
