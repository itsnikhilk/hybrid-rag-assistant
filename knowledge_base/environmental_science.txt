Environmental science studies the relationship between living organisms and their surroundings. It examines ecosystems, biodiversity, natural resources, pollution, climate systems, and environmental conservation. Human activities such as industrial production, deforestation, transportation, and fossil fuel consumption contribute to environmental changes and greenhouse gas emissions.

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns. Increased concentrations of greenhouse gases, including carbon dioxide and methane, contribute to global warming by trapping additional heat in the atmosphere. Renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydropower provide alternatives to fossil fuel-based energy production. Sustainable development focuses on meeting present needs while preserving resources and opportunities for future generations. Environmental monitoring uses satellite imagery, sensors, geographic information systems, and scientific measurements to understand environmental conditions.
