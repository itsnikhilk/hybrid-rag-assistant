Healthcare technology combines medical science with digital tools to improve patient care, diagnosis, treatment, and healthcare management. Electronic
Health Records (EHRs) are digital systems used to store patient information, including medical history, prescriptions, laboratory reports, allergies, and
treatment plans. These systems help healthcare professionals access relevant patient information and coordinate medical services more efficiently.

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being applied in medical imaging, disease prediction, drug discovery, and clinical decision support. Machine learning 
models can analyze medical images such as X-rays, CT scans, and MRI scans to identify patterns that may require further investigation by medical professionals.
Telemedicine allows patients to consult healthcare professionals remotely through video calls and digital communication platforms. Wearable health devices can 
monitor measurements such as heart rate, physical activity, and sleep patterns. Healthcare technology can improve accessibility and operational efficiency, but 
patient privacy, data security, accuracy, and medical professional oversight remain essential.
