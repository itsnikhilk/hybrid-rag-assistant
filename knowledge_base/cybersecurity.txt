Cybersecurity refers to the practices, technologies, and processes used to protect computer systems, networks, applications, and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks, and damage. As organizations increasingly depend on digital infrastructure, cybersecurity has become essential for protecting personal information, financial records, intellectual property, and business operations.

Encryption converts readable information into an encoded format that can only be understood by authorized parties with the appropriate decryption mechanism. Authentication verifies the identity of users attempting to access a system, while authorization determines which resources those users are permitted to access. Firewalls monitor network traffic and apply security rules to control incoming and outgoing connections. Multi-factor authentication adds another verification requirement beyond a password. Common cybersecurity threats include phishing, ransomware, malware, identity theft, and data breaches. Regular software updates, strong authentication, employee awareness, backups, and security monitoring help reduce cybersecurity risks.
