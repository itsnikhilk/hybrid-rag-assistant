Banking and financial technology involve the use of digital platforms and automated systems to manage financial transactions, investments, payments, and banking 
services. Digital banking allows customers to access financial services through mobile applications and websites without visiting physical bank branches. Common
services include money transfers, balance checking, bill payments, account management, and transaction history.

Financial institutions use machine learning and data analytics to identify unusual transaction patterns and detect potential fraud. Credit scoring systems evaluate
financial information to estimate the creditworthiness of individuals and businesses. Algorithmic trading uses computer programs to analyze market information and
execute trading strategies according to predefined rules. Blockchain technology is also used in certain financial applications to maintain distributed and tamper-
resistant transaction records. Security measures such as encryption, multi-factor authentication, access controls, and transaction monitoring help protect financial
information.
