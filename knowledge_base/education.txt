Digital education refers to the use of technology to deliver learning experiences through online platforms, digital resources, and interactive applications.
Learning Management Systems (LMS) help educational institutions organize courses, assignments, assessments, student records, and educational materials.
Students can access lectures, submit assignments, participate in discussions, and monitor their academic progress through these platforms.

Artificial Intelligence is transforming education through personalized learning systems, automated feedback, intelligent tutoring, and educational chatbots.
Adaptive learning platforms analyze student performance and recommend learning materials based on individual progress. Natural Language Processing allows 
educational applications to understand questions and generate explanations in conversational language. Online learning platforms also provide access to courses, 
tutorials, and professional certifications from different locations. However, digital education requires reliable internet access, responsible use of AI-generated
content, and attention to data privacy.
